Data processing agreement

Version {{ brand.dpa_updated }}

Draft, pending legal review. This is the agreement we intend to sign with every customer, published so you can read it before you talk to us rather than after. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. If your own counsel wants changes, that is a conversation and not a problem - and if you have a DPA of your own, send it.

This agreement is between you - the controller - and {{ brand.legal_name }}, the processor. It covers the personal data we process on your behalf when you use {{ brand.name }} as your front desk. It does not cover your own account data, where we are the controller; that is on the data protection page.

1. What we may do with it

Only what you instruct. In practice that means: receive messages sent to your number, answer them, take and change bookings, send reminders and confirmations, pass a conversation to you, and show you all of it. Nothing else. We do not use your customers' data for our own purposes, we do not sell it or share it for advertising, and it is not used to train any model.

If an instruction from you would put us in breach of GDPR, we will tell you instead of carrying it out.

2. How long, and what happens at the end

For as long as you are a customer. When you leave, you can ask us to delete everything or to export it first. We do that within 30 days of the request.

While you are a customer there is no automatic deletion - we keep the conversation history until you ask us to remove it. That is the honest current state and the data protection page says the same.

3. Confidentiality

Only the people who run the service can reach production data, and only to operate and support it. They are bound to confidentiality.

4. Security

Art. 32. Encryption in transit and at rest; strict separation between businesses, enforced on every query and tested on purpose; one-time codes rather than passwords for business sign-in; and a short list of people with production access. The measures in force are described on the data protection page, which is kept current rather than frozen into this text.

5. Sub-processors

You give general authorisation for the sub-processors in the annex below. We will tell you before we add or replace one, and you may object - in which case you can stop using the service and we will refund anything paid in advance. Each of them is under a written agreement no weaker than this one.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

{% if eu_hosted %} The service runs in {{ hosting_location }}, inside the EEA. {% else %} Data is processed in {{ hosting_location }} and elsewhere outside the European Economic Area, as the annex sets out. The transfer mechanism for each is still to be confirmed, which is stated rather than assumed. If EU-only processing is a requirement for you, say so before you sign anything: we are not there yet. {% endif %}

7. Helping you answer your people

When a customer or patient of yours asks you for their data, asks you to correct it, or asks you to delete it, that request is yours to answer and ours to make answerable. Write to {{ brand.dpo_email }} and we will find it, export it or remove it. If one of them contacts us directly we will not answer for you - we will tell them to ask you, and tell you they asked.

8. Breaches, audits and assistance

If there is a breach affecting your data we notify you without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware, with what we know at the time - Art. 33(2). We will give you the information you need for a data protection impact assessment, and we will answer an auditor's questions in writing. An on-site audit is something to agree in advance rather than something this page can promise.

Annex A - what is processed

  • Whose data: your customers, patients or clients, and the people who contact you on their behalf.
  • What: phone number, name if given, the messages themselves, the language used, appointment details, and - where the voice channel is on - call audio and transcripts.
  • Special categories: the assistant does not ask for health information and will not discuss it. What a patient volunteers in their own words may still be Art. 9 data in your hands, and is covered by this agreement. See the clinic section of the data protection page for what the assistant is actually prevented from doing.
  • Why: running your front desk, as section 1 describes.
