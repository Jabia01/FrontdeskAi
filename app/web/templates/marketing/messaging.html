Messaging terms

Last updated {{ brand.legal_updated }}

{{ brand.name }} sends text and WhatsApp messages on behalf of the businesses that use it. This page explains what those messages are, how someone comes to receive them, and how to stop them.

Who the messages come from

Messages are sent from the business you contacted, using their own phone number. {{ brand.name }}, operated by {{ brand.legal_name }}, is the service that sends them on that business's behalf. The first message in any conversation identifies the business by name.

How you come to receive messages

You will only receive messages if one of these is true:

  • You texted the business first. Replying to you is the entire purpose of the service.
  • You called the business and nobody answered. You get one text asking what you need. If you do not reply, that is the end of it.
  • You booked an appointment, so you receive a confirmation and a reminder before it.
  • You asked the business to tell you when your job is done, and they marked it ready.
  • You asked us about using {{ brand.name }} for your own business through the form on our site, so we text you about setting it up.

We do not send marketing or promotional messages, and we do not buy phone numbers from anyone.

How often

Message frequency varies and depends on the conversation. Most people exchange a handful of messages and then nothing until they get in touch again. Automated messages are limited to one reminder per appointment and one notification when a job is ready.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. {{ brand.name }} does not charge you to receive messages; your mobile carrier may.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message. It takes effect immediately and stops everything automated, including appointment reminders. You will get one confirmation that it worked, and then nothing.

STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT all do the same thing. To start again, reply START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message and you will get the business's details and a way to reach a person. You can also email us at {{ brand.support_email }} or call {{ brand.support_phone }}.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your information

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties except subcontractors who help us deliver the service, such as our messaging carrier. Our privacy policy sets this out in full.
