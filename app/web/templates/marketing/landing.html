How it works

Three steps. The middle one is the one nobody else offers.

1

We learn your business

Your hours, services, price ranges and the way you talk to customers. Nothing is invented: if a price isn't on your list, it says so and offers a visit for an exact quote rather than guessing.

2

Two weeks of shadowing

It writes the reply, you approve it on WhatsApp. You see exactly what it would have said to a real customer before a single person hears from it. Change the wording and it sends yours instead.

3

It answers

Text and WhatsApp, day and night. Anything it shouldn't handle - a complaint, an emergency, an insurance question - stops and comes straight to you.

What it does

Answers in their language

Someone writes in Spanish, Polish or English and gets a reply in the same language on the first try. No menus, no "press 1 for English", no asking them to pick.

Texts back missed calls

When a call goes unanswered the caller gets a text within seconds asking what they need - before they try the next business on the list.

Books real appointments

It offers only times that are genuinely free on your calendar, and never holds or promises a slot it hasn't checked.

Reminds them the day before

An automatic reminder in the customer's own language, with the time you actually agreed. Fewer no-shows, and not one phone call from you.

Answers "is it ready yet?"

You text READY once - whatever it was, a car, a dog, a laptop, a suit. The customer is told in their own language the moment they ask.

Knows when to stop

It hands the conversation to you and goes quiet. It will never argue with an angry customer, quote a price you didn't give it, or invent a promise.

One morning summary

What happened yesterday, what's booked today, what's waiting on you. One message, in your language, at the hour you choose.

Runs on your own number

The same number that's on your signs and your van. Your customers never need to know anything changed.

Your customers don't all speak English

Most systems make them choose a language before they can ask a question. {{ brand.name }} reads what they wrote and answers in it - first reply, every time. If they switch mid-conversation, it follows. A first message always includes English, and always tells them they can write in whatever they like.

You never maintain a translated copy of anything. You write your hours and prices once, in your own language, and they come out in theirs.

Questions

Does it pretend to be a person?
No. It says it's your assistant, and it hands over to you the moment a customer asks for a human.
What if it doesn't know something?
It says so and passes the question to you. It can only speak from what you told us - it cannot invent a price, a time or a promise.
Do I need a computer?
No. Everything reaches you on WhatsApp and you reply there. There's a dashboard if you want one, but you never have to open it.
Can I take over a conversation?
Yes. Reply to the alert and {{ brand.name }} goes quiet on that chat until you hand it back. Whatever you type is sent to the customer, translated into their language.
What if I don't like what it says?
That's what the first two weeks are for. It drafts, you approve or rewrite, and we adjust it to sound like you before any customer sees a word.
Which languages?
Whatever your customers actually write in. We switch on the ones your neighbourhood uses, and English is always included.
Do I have to change my phone number?
No. It works on your existing number.
How do I stop it?
Tell us and we switch it off the same day. There's nothing to uninstall.
