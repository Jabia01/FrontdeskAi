How it works

1

We learn your business

Your hours, services, price ranges and the way you talk to customers. Nothing is invented: if a price is not in your list, it says so and offers a visit for an exact quote.

2

Two weeks of shadowing

It writes the reply, you approve it on WhatsApp. You see exactly what it would have said before a single customer hears from it.

3

It answers

Text and WhatsApp, day and night. Anything it should not handle - a complaint, an emergency, an insurance question - goes straight to you.

What it does

Answers in their language

A customer writes in Spanish, Polish or English and gets a reply in the same language on the first try. No menus, no "press 1 for English".

Texts back missed calls

When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text within seconds asking what they need - before they try the next business on the list.

Books real appointments

It offers only times that are genuinely free on your calendar, and never holds a slot it has not checked.

Reminds them the day before

An automatic reminder the day before, in the customer's own language, with the time you actually agreed. Fewer no-shows, no phone calls.

Answers "is it ready yet?"

You text READY once - and whatever it was, a car, a dog, a laptop, a suit. The customer is told in their language the moment they ask.

Knows when to stop

It hands the conversation to you and goes quiet. It will never argue with an angry customer or guess at a price.

Questions

Does it pretend to be a person?
No. It says it is your assistant, and it hands over to you whenever a customer asks for a human.
What if it does not know something?
It says so and passes the question to you. It only speaks from the information you gave us - it cannot invent a price or a promise.
Do I need a computer?
No. Everything reaches you on WhatsApp, and you reply there. This site has a dashboard if you want one, but you never have to open it.
Can I turn it off?
Yes, instantly, and you can take over any single conversation by replying to the alert.
Which languages?
Whatever your customers write in. We enable the ones your neighbourhood actually uses, and English is always included.
