Privacy policy

Last updated {{ brand.legal_updated }}

{{ brand.name }} is operated by {{ brand.legal_name }} ("we", "us"). We provide a service that answers messages on behalf of small businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we will never do with it.

Who the data belongs to

There are two kinds of people in this system, and they are treated differently:

  • Businesses who use {{ brand.name }} as their front desk. They are our customers.
  • The public who message those businesses. Their conversation belongs to the business they contacted. We process it on that business's behalf and for no other purpose.

What we collect

From a business that signs up:

  • Business name, trade, city, contact name, mobile number and email.
  • The information they give us to answer with: opening hours, services, price ranges, policies and tone.
  • Sign-in records for the dashboard, and a log of configuration changes.

From a member of the public who messages a business:

  • Their phone number and the contents of their messages.
  • What the conversation is about - for example a vehicle, a pet or an appointment - as they describe it.
  • The language they write in, so we can reply in it.
  • Appointments booked and whether a job is ready.
  • Records of calls to the business number: the number, the time, and whether it was answered. We do not record call audio.

Your mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except for subcontractors acting as service providers who help us operate the service, such as our messaging carrier.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share phone numbers with lead generators, data brokers, advertisers or affiliates, and we never will.

Why we process it

  • To answer a customer's message on behalf of the business they contacted.
  • To book, confirm and remind about appointments.
  • To tell the business owner when something needs them.
  • To keep the service working - error logs, abuse prevention, and accounting for what the service costs to run.

We do not use anyone's messages to advertise to them, and we do not profile people.

Who we share it with

Only the suppliers needed to deliver the service, and only the minimum each one needs:

  • Our messaging carrier, to deliver texts and WhatsApp messages.
  • Our AI provider, which processes message text to produce a reply. Messages sent for this purpose are not used to train their models.
  • Our hosting and database providers, which store the data at rest.
  • A business's own calendar provider, where that business has connected one, to check availability and write appointments.

We will disclose information where the law requires it. We would tell the affected business first unless we were forbidden from doing so.

How long we keep it

Conversations are kept for as long as the business uses the service, because they are that business's record of what was said to its customers. When a business leaves, we delete or anonymise their data within 90 days, except where we must keep records for legal or accounting reasons.

One exception: where someone has asked us to stop messaging them, we keep the minimum record needed to honour that - otherwise we would have no way to know.

Your choices

  • Stop messages: reply STOP to any message. It takes effect immediately and applies to everything automated, including reminders.
  • Get help: reply HELP, or contact us below.
  • Access, correction or deletion: write to us and we will act on it. If you contacted a business through us, we will work with that business, since the conversation is theirs.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored hashed, never in a form we could read. Dashboard sessions are stored as a hash, so a copy of our database would not let anyone sign in as you. Each business's data is separated at the data layer, and we test that separation automatically on every change.

Children

The service is for businesses. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell businesses directly where the change is significant.
