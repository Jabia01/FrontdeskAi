These terms govern use of {{ brand.name }}, operated by {{ brand.legal_name }}. They are between us and the business using the service. By using {{ brand.name }} you agree to them.

What the service is

{{ brand.name }} answers messages sent to your business, using information you give us, and alerts you when something needs a person. It can book appointments against a calendar you connect, send reminders, and tell customers the status of their job when you set it.

What it is not

It is an assistant, not an employee and not a legal or professional adviser. It answers from the information you provide. It will decline to quote a price you have not given it, and it will hand a conversation to you rather than guess. You remain responsible for what your business tells its customers, including what {{ brand.name }} says on your behalf.

Shadow mode

New accounts start in shadow mode for {{ brand.trial_days }} days: the assistant drafts replies and you approve each one before it is sent. This exists so you can see exactly how it would speak to your customers before it does. You decide when to switch it on.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information about your business - the assistant repeats what you tell it.
  • Keep your prices, hours and services up to date. A wrong price in your configuration becomes a wrong price in a reply.
  • Respond when a conversation is handed to you. The assistant stops; the customer is still waiting.
  • Use the service only for your own business, and only for lawful messages.
  • Comply with the messaging rules that apply to you, including registration requirements for business texting in your country.

What you must not do

  • Send marketing or promotional messages through the service.
  • Message people who have not contacted you or asked to hear from you.
  • Upload numbers you bought, scraped or were given by a lead seller.
  • Present the assistant as a human being if asked directly.
  • Use it for anything the law, your carrier or common decency forbids.

We will suspend an account that does any of these, and we will do it without much ceremony - this kind of use gets phone numbers blocked for everyone.

It depends on third parties - messaging carriers, an AI provider, hosting - and any of them can have an outage. If the assistant cannot answer, it says so and alerts you rather than leaving a customer in silence.
